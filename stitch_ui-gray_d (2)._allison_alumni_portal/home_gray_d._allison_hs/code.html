Institutional Profile

A Legacy of Discipline and Excellence

Founded under the auspices of the Ministry of National Defense, Gray D. Allison High School has stood as a bastion of academic rigor and character building for decades. Our institution is dedicated to nurturing the next generation of Liberian leaders through a curriculum that balances intellectual pursuit with civic responsibility.

The Alumni & Information Management System (AIMS) is our digital leap forward, ensuring that every graduate remains connected to their alma mater and has seamless access to their essential academic credentials.

How to Request Your Documents

A simple 3-step process to access your academic history.

1

Identity Verification

Register on the AIMS portal using your graduating class details and a valid national ID.

2

Select Document

Choose the specific document you need (Transcript, Certificate, or Diploma) and provide delivery details.

3

Secure Processing

Pay the processing fee securely online and track your document status until delivery.
